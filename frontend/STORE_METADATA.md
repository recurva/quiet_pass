## Full description

Living with housemates means constant small questions: Is anyone free to talk? Is the living room free tonight? Who's home for dinner? QuietPass answers all of it at a glance, without another group chat to scroll through.

**Know who's around, without asking**
Set your status — Open to Chat, Deep Focus, In Call, Sleeping Early, Away — with an optional timer, so your house always knows whether now's a good time.

**Dinner, sorted by 5pm**
A simple daily tally: tap Home for Dinner or Staying Out. See the count and who's in, right on your house's home screen.

**Book shared spaces, including every week**
Reserve the living room, a desk, or any shared space for a one-off slot or a recurring weekly booking — with automatic clean-up reminders tied to every booking, and a built-in buffer so bookings never collide.

**Nudge the house, anonymously**
Send a quick, anonymous alert — package arrived, sink's full, front door's unlocked, or your own message — without it turning into a conversation.

**Urgent access, politely**
Need a space someone else is using right now, just for five minutes? Send a polite request straight to them — never an automatic takeover.

**House agreements, always findable**
Pin the trash schedule, quiet hours, the landlord's number, or any house rule somewhere everyone can actually find it.

**Guest Wi-Fi, the easy way**
Generate a QR code for guests to scan with their own camera — no reading out a password, no auto-connecting anyone, just a quick scan and they're on.

QuietPass is built for the day-to-day reality of sharing a home: quick to check, quick to update, and never louder than it needs to be.
